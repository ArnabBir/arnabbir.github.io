    Evidence Rubric¶

    Practice home | Exemplars | Interviewer packet

    Score demonstrated evidence, not product names. Every dimension receives 0-4 points. Apply gates and then the lowest applicable cap.

    Scoring model¶

    Score Evidence standard
    0 Missing, contradictory, or unsafe
    1 Names a concern without an enforceable mechanism
    2 Coherent normal path with material failure or measurement gaps
    3 Correct mechanism, explicit bound, failure behavior, and proof signal
    4 Level 3 plus quantified sensitivity, evolution, or fleet governance judgment

    Dimensions¶

    A. Decision contract, risk, and authority¶

    • Defines responder decisions, answerability, SLOs, abstention, harms, and human accountability.
    • Separates evidence, inference, recommendation, approval, and effect.
    • Makes all production mutations deny-by-default behind current scoped approval.

    B. Workload, context, fanout, and economics¶

    • Correctly calculates turn rate, tool demand, retry sensitivity, context fit, and fixture cost.
    • Uses progressive fanout, context allocation, admission, fairness, and hard budget ceilings.
    • Connects sensitivity and provider/region reserve to operating limits.

    C. Evidence ingestion, provenance, and lifecycle¶

    • Preserves source/version, event/ingest time, query/content digest, transforms, ACL, classification, retention, and lineage.
    • Handles late data, clock uncertainty, schema/parser versions, quarantine, revocation, deletion, and derived artifacts.
    • Supports deterministic post-incident reconstruction without rerunning mutable dependencies.

    D. Retrieval, ranking, grounding, and stale runbooks¶

    • Defines authorized structured/lexical/vector candidates, candidate ceilings, fusion, reranking, diversity, and direct metrics.
    • Ranks source authority, freshness, applicability, and contradictions; treats runbooks as versioned guidance.
    • Requires material claims to cite support and exposes uncertainty or abstention.

    E. Workflow, MCP, timeout, and idempotency¶

    • Bounds workflow steps, calls, attempts, deadlines, queues, output, and retries.
    • Defines typed, versioned, least-privilege tools with logical/attempt identity.
    • Handles timeout-after-effect as unknown using stable argument-bound keys, reconciliation, replay, and safe compensation.

    F. Security, service authorization, privacy, and approval¶

    • Enforces principal/service/resource policy before retrieval and through caches, summaries, exports, and revocation.
    • Treats all content as untrusted; protects credentials, limits egress, redacts outputs, and records denials.
    • Approval binds incident, approver, scope, action, arguments, preconditions, expiry, risk, and usage; execution rechecks all fields.

    G. Incident-mode reliability and regional/provider degradation¶

    • Preserves safe deterministic evidence paths when model or telemetry dependencies degrade.
    • Defines fail-closed boundaries for identity/audit uncertainty, bounded partial responses, provider routing, regional state, and recovery.
    • Quantifies receiver headroom and correlated failures; provides measurable entry and exit criteria.

    H. Observability, audit, and operational response¶

    • Separates logical investigations, steps, calls, attempts, model routes, recommendations, approvals, and effects.
    • Defines stage, safety, budget, provenance, unknown-outcome, reconstruction, and audit-backlog signals.
    • Gives detection, reversible containment, recovery, kill switch, runbooks, ownership, and proof of exit.

    I. Evaluation, release, and adversarial verification¶

    • Measures retrieval, ranking, support, hypotheses, mitigation safety, authorization, latency, cost, and outcomes separately.
    • Uses versioned representative sets, hard slices, human judgments, calibrated judges, confidence, counterfactuals, and contamination controls.
    • Defines canary, shadowing, fault/adversarial tests, fixed gates, rollback, and delayed-outcome review.

    J. Migration, compatibility, governance, and ownership¶

    • Gives staged authority, shadow comparison, generation pinning, compatibility, promotion, rollback, and retirement evidence.
    • Names owners and decision rights for policy, tools, incidents, data, evaluation, capacity, cost, and exceptions.
    • Establishes fleet contracts and evidence-based revisit triggers.

    Maximum: 40 points.

    Critical caps¶

    Apply the lowest relevant cap after normalization.

    Defect Maximum outcome
    Gives the model production credentials, allows unapproved mutation, or accepts chat/runbook text as approval 11/40; below L5
    Retrieves private evidence without principal/service authorization before candidate generation, or leaks it through caches/summaries 17/40; below L5
    Treats timeout as proof no effect occurred, changes arguments on retry, or claims exactly-once from transport/idempotency alone 17/40; below L5
    Uses unbounded fanout, context, workflow steps, retries, queues, or spend 23/40; weak L5
    Cannot cite claim provenance or reconstruct approval and effect history 23/40; weak L5
    Treats runbooks/tool output as trusted instructions or disables security/audit controls in incident mode 23/40; weak L5
    Has no direct retrieval metric, correct quantitative calculation, fixed safety slice, or rollback/kill switch 27/40; borderline L5
    At 45/60 minutes, has no credible correlated provider/region degradation or audit recovery 32/40; strong L5
    At 60 minutes, has no compatible migration, fleet policy, exception governance, economics, or accountable ownership 35/40; borderline L6

    Level calibration¶

    Outcome Normalized total Required pattern
    Below target 0-17 Unsafe authority, isolation, or effect semantics, or central design is missing
    Weak L5 18-23 Useful normal path with major provenance, budget, evaluation, or incident gaps
    Borderline L5 24-27 Coherent bounded design with one important safety or recovery boundary weak
    Strong L5 28-32 Independently safe, quantified, observable, evaluable, and recoverable
    Borderline L6 33-35 Strong L5 plus platform evolution and regional judgment with one leadership gap
    Strong L6 36-40 Establishes fleet policy, economics, compatible evolution, and accountable evidence

    L5 gates¶

    • States answerability, safety invariants, model/tool authority, and explicit approval boundary.
    • Traces one request through authorization, retrieval, ranking, context, model, validation, and response with versions.
    • Computes at least four values covering context, retrieval, fanout/reliability, and cost/capacity.
    • Bounds context, calls, steps, deadlines, attempts, queues, output, and spend.
    • Handles timeout-after-effect with stable identity and reconciliation.
    • Preserves provenance, service authorization, retention, revocation, and deletion through derivatives.
    • Measures retrieval, grounding, safety, latency, and cost separately with release and rollback gates.
    • Defines a safe incident mode and append-only audit reconstruction.

    L6 gates¶

    • Satisfies every L5 gate without interviewer repair.
    • Quantifies correlated provider/region loss, receiver headroom, and fallback economics.
    • Defines compatible prompt/model/index/tool/policy/audit migrations and rollback authority.
    • Establishes fleet policy, governed time-bounded exceptions, owners, and retirement evidence.
    • Names falsifying evidence, its owner, retention, review cadence, and decision consequence.

    Evidence capture sheet¶

    Dimension Score Exact candidate evidence Missing proof or contradiction
    A. Decision/authority
    B. Budgets/economics
    C. Provenance/lifecycle
    D. Retrieval/grounding
    E. Workflow/tools
    F. Security/approval
    G. Incident/regions
    H. Operations/audit
    I. Evaluation/release
    J. Migration/governance

    Record: subtotal/available -> normalized/40 (mode), L5/L6 gates, and lowest cap.

    Mode normalization¶

    Calculate normalized = subtotal / available points * 40, round to the nearest whole point, then apply caps. Normalization compares evidence within a mode; it does not claim omitted dimensions were demonstrated.

    Resolve exact halves upward with floor(subtotal / available_points * 40 + 0.5). Apply caps to demonstrated unsafe behavior even outside the mode's scored dimensions, not to evidence omitted solely by that mode. In 15/30-minute modes, report scope-limited calibration against the mode's required evidence; do not claim all full-design L5 gates passed. Full-design L5/L6 certification requires the corresponding gates, separately from the numeric score. The local 40-point score is not interchangeable with the track's 100-point score.

    The normalized number compares attempts within the selected mode. It does not award an L6 calibration: only a 60-minute attempt that scores A-J and satisfies the L6 migration, regional economics, governance, ownership, and falsification gates may be labeled Borderline or Strong L6. A shorter-mode result is calibrated no higher than Strong L5 even when normalization is 36-40.

    Mode Scored dimensions Available Additional required evidence
    15 minutes A-D 16 One correct budget calculation and one failure trace
    30 minutes A-F 24 One tool-timeout trace and one authorization test
    45 minutes A-I 36 Audit reconstruction, incident mode, and evaluation gates
    60 minutes A-J 40 Migration, regional economics, governance, and ownership

    Example: 19/24 -> 32/40 (30-minute mode). This is strong L5 evidence for the selected scope, not proof of L6 migration depth.

    Debrief rule¶

    Give one demonstrated strength, one concrete highest-risk failure sequence, the exact evidence needed to close it, and one next-level exercise. Never average a production-authority defect into a passing score.
